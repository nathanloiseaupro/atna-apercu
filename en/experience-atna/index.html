The ATNA experience

A turnkey project, managed by a single team, from the plot to the first dinner in your home.

The site

Before any commitment, we study the plot: its orientation, its access and what the planning rules allow. If you don’t have a plot yet, we study the ones you are considering with you.

The design

We design the project with you: plans, volumes, materials and light. You receive a sketch design, then a detailed budget, and you approve each stage.

The permits

We prepare the planning applications and follow them through to approval.

The construction

We select the contractors, coordinate the works and keep you informed of progress through to handover.

Interior and outdoors

Fittings, dressing rooms, furniture, decoration, terraces, garden and lighting: we choose them with you, room by room.

The handover

Linen, tableware and the final details are in place when you arrive. On the first evening, a chef can cook at your home, as an option.
