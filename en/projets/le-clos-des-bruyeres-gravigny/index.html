Le Clos des Bruyères, Gravigny

Housing · Gravigny, Normandy

Category
Housing
Status
Completed
Location
Gravigny, Normandy

Fifty-three high energy performance homes, including thirty-five houses and two small apartment buildings, on a former brownfield site.

The project was selected through a competition in 2009, then built. Each house combines white and charcoal grey volumes, with its own garden and garage.

Construction cost: 5 million euros excluding tax.
