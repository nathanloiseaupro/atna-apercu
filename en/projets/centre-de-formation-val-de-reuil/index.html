Training centre, Val-de-Reuil

Public building · Val-de-Reuil, Normandy

Category

Extension of the dining hall and restructuring of a 9,550 m² training centre, completed in 2012.

A fully glazed façade, sheltered by a wide white canopy, opens the dining hall onto the garden.

Construction cost: 2.2 million euros excluding tax.
