Your questions

Another question? We will answer it at our first meeting.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

ATNA & Associés is an architecture, interior architecture and project management firm. We design new contemporary villas, we renovate and restore houses of character (country estates, manor houses, old homes), and we deliver commercial buildings and hotel projects. We favour bioclimatic construction: timber frames, bio-based insulation, solar energy and rainwater harvesting.

What does a project managed from start to finish mean?

One team manages your project, so you have no building-site constraints to deal with. We study the plot and the planning rules, we design the project (plans, volumes, materials, 3D images), we prepare and follow up the building permit, we select the craftsmen and direct the works, then we design bespoke furniture and choose the decoration, down to the household linen and tableware. You move into a furnished home that is ready to live in. For the first evening, a private chef can cook for you and your guests, as an option.

How much does a turnkey project cost?

Every project is designed to measure. Its budget depends on the floor area, the complexity of the architecture, the plot and the level of finish: bespoke joinery, home automation, energy self-sufficiency equipment, landscaping. From the sketch stage, we draw up a detailed cost estimate. Our dual expertise as architect and construction economist then lets us keep a close eye on that budget, from signature to handover.

I don’t have a plot yet. Can you help?

Yes. With our network of partners in France and abroad (property finders, notaries, land specialists), we help you find and choose your plot. Before any purchase, we study its topography, its orientation and the planning rules that apply, to check that your project can be built there. We also offer plots paired with a house design.

Do I have to choose the furniture and decoration myself?

You choose how involved you want to be. We present complete proposals: mood boards, material samples, designer furniture, plans for bespoke bathrooms and dressing rooms. You approve our selections, then we handle the orders, delivery and installation, tableware and household linen included.

Where do you work?

With more than 25 years of experience and 120 million euros of completed projects, we work with clients in France and abroad. In France: Brittany, Paris and the Île-de-France region, the Atlantic coast, Normandy and the French Riviera. Abroad: Morocco, where we have three offices, Portugal (Lisbon, Madeira, the Algarve), Spain and Italy. We also consider projects anywhere else in the world.

Do you use ecological materials?

Yes, sustainability is at the heart of our projects. Our team includes an engineer in sustainable development, and we design buildings suited to a changing climate. We favour bio-based and natural materials (timber frames, straw, cellulose wadding, wood fibre, lime, stone) for a healthy, comfortable interior, and we discreetly integrate solar panels, battery storage and rainwater harvesting.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scale of the project and the time taken to process the building permit. As a rule, allow 3 to 5 months for design and permits, then 10 to 18 months for the works, fit-out and handover. As soon as the design studies begin, we give you a detailed schedule, which we follow closely through to handover.
