Your questions

Another question? We will answer it at our first meeting.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Private residences, renovations and extensions, residential and commercial buildings, and public facilities. We work on new builds as well as existing buildings.

What does a project managed from A to Z mean?

We take charge of every stage: the site study, the design, the works, the interior, the outdoors, then the furniture, linen and tableware. You have a single point of contact and you keep the final say on every choice.

How much does a turnkey project cost?

The budget depends on the site, the floor area and the level of finish. We give you an initial estimate after visiting the site, then a detailed budget with the sketch design.

I don’t have a plot yet. Can you help?

Yes. We study the plots you are considering with you and tell you what can be built on them before you commit. We also offer plots paired with a house design.

Do I have to choose the furniture and decoration myself?

No. We select them with you, room by room, down to the household linen and tableware. You approve every choice.

Where do you work?

In Brittany, Paris, on the Atlantic coast, in Normandy and on the French Riviera. Abroad, in Morocco, where we have three offices, as well as in Portugal, Spain and Italy.

Do you use environmentally friendly materials?

Yes. On every project, we offer bio-based materials and design energy-efficient buildings.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the nature of the project, the planning permissions and the works. We give you a schedule with the sketch design.

Let’s talk about your project

Tell us where your plot is and what you have in mind. We will call you back to discuss it.
