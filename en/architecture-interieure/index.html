Interior architecture and design

We design the interior at the same time as the building, so that every room is thought through down to how it is used.

Fittings

Kitchens, dressing rooms, bathrooms and storage are custom designed, in keeping with the architecture.

Furniture and decoration

We select the furniture, lighting, textiles and objects with you, room by room. You approve every choice.

Linen and tableware

For a home that is ready to live in, we also provide household linen and tableware, in place before you arrive.

The outdoors

Terraces, pool, garden and lighting extend the interior and are designed together with it.
