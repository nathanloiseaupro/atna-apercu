Houses for sale

A chosen plot, and a house designed for it.

  1. A selected plot

    We select plots for their location, their orientation and what the planning rules allow to be built on them.

  2. A house design

    For each plot, we design a house suited to the site. You discover the location and the sketch.

  3. A project tailored to you

    We then adapt the plans, materials and interior to the way you live, and manage the project through to the handover of the keys.
